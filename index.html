Frequently asked questions.

When does the competition start?

The competition launches with Season 01. Join the waitlist and we’ll send you the entry challenge as soon as it goes live.

Who can participate?

Anyone who can solve the entry challenge. There are no prerequisites beyond that — your solution is your application.

Is participation free?

Yes. There is no fee to participate. The competition is organized and funded by Second Foundation.

How long does a challenge take?

It depends on the challenge. Online challenges tend to be shorter with strict time limits. In-person rounds allow more time for deeper problems.

How does scoring work?

Each scored challenge awards points based on performance. Your seasonal total determines your leaderboard position. Details are shared with each challenge.

Can I participate under a nickname?

Yes. Your real identity stays with the platform. On the leaderboard, you compete under a nickname of your choice.

Can I use AI?

Every challenge clearly states whether AI tools are allowed. In-person competitive rounds are solved without AI.

Do I need to attend in-person rounds?

Online challenges can be solved remotely. Some later stages of the competition include in-person rounds for those who progress far enough.

What exactly can I win?

The main prize is a conference trip to San Francisco, including a ticket and flights. Along the way, you can earn badges, reputation, access to events, expert feedback, and more.

Who creates the challenges?

The first challenges are created by Second Foundation. Over time, top solvers may earn the opportunity to create challenges themselves.

Is this a recruitment process?

No. The competition is a standalone platform. You don’t need to be interested in working at Second Foundation to participate.
